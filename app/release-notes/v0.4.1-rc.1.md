**Pre-release** of 0.4.1, for testing before it becomes the latest version. 0.4.0 stays the latest until then.

## Fixes

- **Chinese text written into a document no longer loops.** After writing and saving the right text, the planner could choose to "replace" one number in it with the whole text, and DeskMind spliced that in, so the document nested inside itself and every later step rewrote a longer copy. Now such a step counts as rewriting the field; when the field already says that, nothing changes, and the task finishes. On our benchmark task for this (G04), 3 of 3 runs finish in 4 steps, against 20 steps and 2 of 6 before. The whole diag suite passes 39 of 39 (diag-v27).
- **On a French (AZERTY) or Dvorak keyboard, select-all and paste press the right keys.** In apps read from the screen, DeskMind pastes with cmd+A and cmd+V, sent as the key positions of a US keyboard. On AZERTY that position types Q, so cmd+A could quit the app; on Dvorak, cmd+V was cmd+K. The keys now come from your keyboard layout.
- **No crash when the helper is stopped while quitting.** DeskMind Hands could crash if macOS stopped it at the moment it was quitting, and leave the model server running. It now stops cleanly and takes the model server with it.

## New

- **Save Full Log…** next to "Report on GitHub" after a failed run. It saves a .zip on your Mac with the run's steps, a screenshot of each step, the model servers' logs and the error. It holds what was on your screen, file names and your account name, so look through it before you share it; DeskMind sends nothing.
- **Reports open a GitHub form already filled in:** what you asked, what happened (with the steps and a folded diagnostics section), the app version and macOS. Check it and submit it yourself.
- **View › Smooth Recordings (30 fps, Slows Tasks About 10%).** Recording a task now takes a picture ten times a second instead of running a capture stream. On 40 real decisions of the local model, recording made each decision about 3% slower, against about 10% with the stream. Turn this on for footage where smooth motion matters.

## Changes

- A mistake in the apps file (`~/.config/deskmind/apps.yaml`, which you write only to configure apps by hand) is now reported by name ("unknown keys: groundings…"), instead of a generic error.
- "It guessed instead of asking" reports link the issue that collects those cases.
- For developers running DeskMind Hands from a terminal: runs no longer leave Dock tiles behind.
- The models are the same as in 0.4.0, and models already downloaded are not downloaded again.

## Requirements

A Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.

**中文说明**

0.4.1 的**预发布版**，转为正式版之前供测试。在此之前，正式版仍是 0.4.0。

**修复**
- **往文档里写中文不再死循环。** 以前写好并保存正确内容后，模型可能选择把其中一个数字「替换」成整段文字，DeskMind 照做，文档就嵌套进了自己，之后每一步都改写一份更长的副本。现在这种操作按「整段改写」处理；内容已经是这样时不做改动，任务随之完成。在专测这个问题的评测任务（G04）上，3 次运行都在 4 步内完成（以前用满 20 步，6 次只过 2 次）。整套 diag 评测 39 题全部通过（diag-v27）。
- **使用法语（AZERTY）或 Dvorak 键盘时，全选和粘贴会按对键。** 对靠屏幕识别的应用，DeskMind 用 cmd+A、cmd+V 粘贴，以前按的是美式键盘上的键位。在 AZERTY 上那个键位是 Q，cmd+A 可能直接退出应用；在 Dvorak 上 cmd+V 变成了 cmd+K。现在按你的键盘布局找对应的键。
- **后台助手在退出时被系统停止，不再崩溃。** 以前 DeskMind Hands 正在退出时如果被 macOS 停止，可能崩溃，并留下没关掉的模型服务。现在会正常退出，并把模型服务一起关掉。

**新功能**
- **运行失败后，「在 GitHub 上反馈」旁边新增「保存完整日志…」。** 在你的 Mac 上存一个 .zip，里面有这次运行的步骤、每一步的截图、模型服务的日志和错误原文。它包含你屏幕上的内容、文件名和账户名，分享前请先检查；DeskMind 不会发送任何内容。
- **反馈会打开已填好的 GitHub 表单：** 你让它做什么、实际发生了什么（含步骤和折叠的诊断信息）、App 版本和 macOS 版本。你检查后自己提交。
- **「显示 › 流畅录制（每秒 30 帧，任务约慢 10%）」。** 录制任务现在每秒截 10 张图，不再开持续录屏流。在本地模型的 40 次真实决策上实测，开着录制时每次决策慢约 3%（录屏流约 10%）。需要画面流畅的录像时再打开这个选项。

**其他**
- 应用配置文件（`~/.config/deskmind/apps.yaml`，只有手动配置应用时才会写）里有写错的字段时，现在会直接指出是哪个字段，而不是笼统地报错。
- 「它没问就猜了」的反馈会链接到收集这类情况的 issue。
- 面向开发者：在终端里运行 DeskMind Hands 时，不再在 Dock 里留下图标。
- 模型与 0.4.0 相同，已下载的模型不会重新下载。

**运行要求**：Apple Silicon 芯片的 Mac，macOS 15 或更高版本。
