**Pre-release** of 0.4.1, for testing before it becomes the latest version. 0.4.0 stays the latest until then.

## Since rc.2

- **An approval covers one step.** When DeskMind asks before a step that may not be undoable (deleting, sending, paying, publishing, sharing), your yes covers that step and the confirmation it opens. Before, it could also cover another step of the same kind a step or two later in the same app: approving one deletion could let a second, different item be deleted without asking.
- **DeskMind acts only on what it offered the model.** It now checks every answer against the options it actually offered. An answer outside them stops the run with the reason, instead of being turned into one of the options.

Everything in rc.2 is included; see the 0.4.1-rc.2 notes.

**中文说明**

0.4.1 的**预发布版**，转为正式版之前供测试。在此之前，正式版仍是 0.4.0。

**相比 rc.2**
- **一次批准只管一步。** DeskMind 在可能无法撤销的步骤（删除、发送、付款、发布、分享）之前会先问你。你的同意只覆盖这一步，以及这一步自己弹出的确认框。以前，同一个 App 里隔一两步的另一个同类步骤也可能沿用这次批准：批准删除一项后，另一项可能不经询问就被删掉。
- **DeskMind 只执行它给模型提供过的选项。** 现在每个回答都会和实际提供的选项核对。超出范围的回答会让这次运行停下并说明原因，不会被硬套成某个选项。

rc.2 的全部内容都包含在内，见 0.4.1-rc.2 的说明。
