**Pre-release** of 0.4.1, for testing before it becomes the latest version. 0.4.0 stays the latest until then.

## Since rc.1

- **Screen Recording is recognised as soon as you turn it on.** On a Mac setting DeskMind up for the first time, the setup could keep saying Screen Recording was off after it was turned on, and quitting and reopening DeskMind didn't help. macOS's own "Quit & Reopen" had started a copy of DeskMind Hands whose permission didn't count. DeskMind now always runs the right copy, and restarts it by itself once the permission is granted.
- **The background helper has a Restart button** in the setup.
- **The app follows your system language** unless you've chosen one in the language menu. It used to start in English everywhere.
- **The whole setup fits:** the examples appear once setup is done, so the last setup step is no longer pushed out of sight, and long descriptions are no longer cut off.

Everything in rc.1 is included; see the 0.4.1-rc.1 notes.

**中文说明**

0.4.1 的**预发布版**，转为正式版之前供测试。在此之前，正式版仍是 0.4.0。

**相比 rc.1**
- **打开「屏幕录制」后马上生效。** 在第一次设置 DeskMind 的 Mac 上，打开屏幕录制权限后，设置里可能仍显示未开启，退出重开 DeskMind 也没用：macOS 自带的「退出并重新打开」启动了一个权限不算数的 DeskMind Hands 副本。现在 DeskMind 总是运行正确的那一份，并在权限打开后自动重启它。
- **设置里的「后台助手」一行新增了「重启」按钮。**
- **App 默认跟随系统语言**，除非你在语言菜单里选过。以前在任何系统上都默认英文。
- **设置能完整显示：** 示例在设置完成后才出现，最后一步设置不会再被挤到看不见的地方，较长的说明也不再被截断。

rc.1 的全部内容都包含在内，见 0.4.1-rc.1 的说明。
