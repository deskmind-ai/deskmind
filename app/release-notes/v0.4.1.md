DeskMind for Mac 0.4.1 fixes what the first week turned up: setting up on a new Mac, a document that kept growing, keyboard layouts other than US, and how far an approval reaches.

## Fixes

- **Screen Recording is recognised as soon as you turn it on.** On a Mac setting DeskMind up for the first time, the setup could keep saying Screen Recording was off after it was turned on, and quitting and reopening DeskMind didn't help. macOS's own "Quit & Reopen" had started a copy of DeskMind Hands whose permission didn't count. DeskMind now always runs the right copy, and restarts it by itself once the permission is granted.
- **Writing into a document no longer loops.** After writing and saving the right text, the model could choose to "replace" part of it with the whole text, and DeskMind spliced that in, so the document nested inside itself and every later step wrote a longer copy. Such a step now rewrites the field; when the field already says that, nothing changes, and the task finishes. On our benchmark task for this (G04), 3 of 3 runs finish in 4 steps, against 20 steps and 2 of 6 before. The whole diag suite passes 39 of 39 (diag-v27).
- **An approval covers one step.** When DeskMind asks before a step that may not be undoable (deleting, sending, paying, publishing, sharing), your yes covers that step and the confirmation it opens. Before, it could also cover another step of the same kind a step or two later in the same app: approving one deletion could let a second, different item be deleted without asking.
- **DeskMind acts only on what it offered the model.** Every answer is checked against the options DeskMind actually offered. An answer outside them stops the run with the reason, instead of being turned into one of the options.
- **On a French (AZERTY) or Dvorak keyboard, select-all and paste press the right keys.** In apps read from the screen, DeskMind used the key positions of a US keyboard: on AZERTY, cmd+A could arrive as cmd+Q and quit the app; on Dvorak, cmd+V was cmd+K. The keys now come from your keyboard layout. Checked on a Mac with the French layout.
- **No crash when the helper is stopped while quitting.** DeskMind Hands could crash if macOS stopped it at the moment it was quitting, and leave the model server running. It now stops cleanly and takes the model server with it.

## New

- **A Restart button for the background helper**, in the setup.
- **Save Full Log…** next to "Report on GitHub" after a failed run. It saves a .zip on your Mac with the run's steps, a screenshot of each step, the model servers' logs and the error. It holds what was on your screen, file names and your account name, so look through it before you share it; DeskMind sends nothing.
- **Reports open a GitHub form already filled in:** what you asked, what happened (with the steps and a folded diagnostics section), the app version and macOS. Check it and submit it yourself.
- **View › Smooth Recordings (30 fps, Slows Tasks About 10%).** Recording a task now takes a picture ten times a second instead of running a capture stream, which makes each decision about 3% slower instead of about 10%. Turn this on for footage where smooth motion matters.

## Changes

- **The app follows your system language** unless you've chosen one in the language menu. It used to start in English everywhere.
- **The whole setup fits on screen:** the examples appear once setup is done, and long descriptions are no longer cut off.
- A mistake in the apps file (`~/.config/deskmind/apps.yaml`, which you write only to configure apps by hand) is reported by name.
- The models are the same as in 0.4.0, and models already downloaded are not downloaded again.

## Known

- In Notes, DeskMind can't yet select a note in the list, so tasks on a single note don't work there (deskmind#49).

## Requirements

A Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.

**中文说明**

DeskMind for Mac 0.4.1 修复了第一周发现的问题：新 Mac 上的设置、越写越长的文档、非美式键盘布局，以及一次批准能管多远。

**修复**
- **打开「屏幕录制」后马上生效。** 在第一次设置 DeskMind 的 Mac 上，打开屏幕录制后，设置里可能仍显示未开启，退出重开 DeskMind 也没用：macOS 自带的「退出并重新打开」启动了一个权限不算数的 DeskMind Hands 副本。现在 DeskMind 总是运行正确的那一份，并在权限打开后自动重启它。
- **往文档里写内容不再死循环。** 以前写好并保存正确内容后，模型可能选择把其中一部分「替换」成整段文字，DeskMind 照做，文档就嵌套进了自己，之后每一步都写一份更长的副本。现在这种操作按「整段改写」处理；内容已经是这样时不做改动，任务随之完成。在专测这个问题的评测任务（G04）上，3 次运行都在 4 步内完成（以前用满 20 步，6 次只过 2 次）。整套 diag 评测 39 题全部通过（diag-v27）。
- **一次批准只管一步。** DeskMind 在可能无法撤销的步骤（删除、发送、付款、发布、分享）之前会先问你。你的同意只覆盖这一步，以及这一步自己弹出的确认框。以前，同一个 App 里隔一两步的另一个同类步骤也可能沿用这次批准：批准删除一项后，另一项可能不经询问就被删掉。
- **DeskMind 只执行它给模型提供过的选项。** 每个回答都会和实际提供的选项核对。超出范围的回答会让这次运行停下并说明原因，不会被硬套成某个选项。
- **使用法语（AZERTY）或 Dvorak 键盘时，全选和粘贴会按对键。** 对靠屏幕识别的应用，DeskMind 以前按美式键盘的键位发送：在 AZERTY 上 ⌘A 可能变成 ⌘Q，直接退出应用；在 Dvorak 上 ⌘V 变成了 ⌘K。现在按你的键盘布局找对应的键。已在法语键盘布局的 Mac 上实测。
- **后台助手在退出时被系统停止，不再崩溃。** 以前 DeskMind Hands 正在退出时如果被 macOS 停止，可能崩溃，并留下没关掉的模型服务。现在会正常退出，并把模型服务一起关掉。

**新功能**
- **设置里的「后台助手」一行新增「重启」按钮。**
- **运行失败后，「在 GitHub 上反馈」旁边新增「保存完整日志…」。** 在你的 Mac 上存一个 .zip，里面有这次运行的步骤、每一步的截图、模型服务的日志和错误原文。它包含你屏幕上的内容、文件名和账户名，分享前请先检查；DeskMind 不会发送任何内容。
- **反馈会打开已填好的 GitHub 表单：** 你让它做什么、实际发生了什么（含步骤和折叠的诊断信息）、App 版本和 macOS 版本。你检查后自己提交。
- **「显示 › 流畅录制（每秒 30 帧，任务约慢 10%）」。** 录制任务现在每秒截 10 张图，不再开持续录屏流，每次决策约慢 3%（录屏流约 10%）。需要画面流畅的录像时再打开这个选项。

**其他**
- **App 默认跟随系统语言**，除非你在语言菜单里选过。以前在任何系统上都默认英文。
- **设置页能完整显示：** 示例在设置完成后才出现，较长的说明也不再被截断。
- `~/.config/deskmind/apps.yaml`（只在手动配置应用时才需要写）里写错的字段，现在会直接指出来。
- 模型与 0.4.0 相同，已经下载的模型不会重复下载。

**已知问题**
- 在「备忘录」里，DeskMind 暂时还不能在列表里选中某条备忘录，所以针对单条备忘录的任务做不了（deskmind#49）。

**系统要求**：Apple 芯片的 Mac，macOS 15 或更新版本。
